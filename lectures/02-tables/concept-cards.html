Two rules worth memorizing

Both do the most damage when nothing looks wrong.

① The default is inner — forget how= and unmatched rows are deleted without a word.
② An empty key never matches anything — not even another empty key.
Our item_id is empty on ~45% of rows → an inner join throws away half the table.
Counting catches two failures out of three: count rows before and after — assert len(after) == len(before)
A wrong key leaves the count intact. Only you know what a column means.
Coming from Excel? A join is VLOOKUP, minus two guarantees.
VLOOKUP returns the first match; a join returns every match.
VLOOKUP only adds columns; a join can also delete rows.
